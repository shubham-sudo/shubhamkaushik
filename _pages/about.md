I'm a PhD student at Brandeis University, advised by [Prof. Subhadeep Sarkar](https://subhadeep.net/), working on storage systems and database internals. My current project, FluidLSM, is about making storage engines smarter — systems that adapt automatically to changing conditions instead of requiring manual tuning or restarts. Before grad school, I spent four years as a software engineer, and that experience still shapes how I approach research. Always happy to discuss ideas, brainstorm over system problems, or explore collaborations — feel free to [reach out](mailto:kaushiks@brandeis.edu).
